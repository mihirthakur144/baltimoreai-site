Case Summary — relevant law (6 docs · fictional)

Every law, statute, and precedent the assistant can cite, summarized in plain English:

📄 Halloway v. Pierce Logistics Force majeure clauses are read narrowly: a fire at one facility doesn't excuse missing all your supply obligations unless the clause explicitly covers single-facility events.
📄 Md. Com. Law § 14-302 (Sample) If a commercial contract doesn't set its own late-payment rate, the default is 1% monthly interest starting 60 days out, once written notice has been given.
📄 In re Vantage Retail Group A landlord's generic "no assignment without consent" clause doesn't automatically block a lease assignment during Chapter 11 — it has to specifically address insolvency to survive.
📄 Firm Memo — Non-Compete Standards Non-competes under 24 months, reasonable in scope, and tied to a real business interest are enforceable. Overbroad ones are usually narrowed by the court rather than thrown out entirely.
📄 Turner v. Bright Path Clinics In a retaliation claim, once the employee makes a plausible case, the employer must give a legitimate reason for the termination — and the employee then gets a chance to show that reason is pretextual.
📄 Firm Client Intake Policy Run a 7-year conflict check before opening any new matter. If a former client sits on the other side, both parties need to sign a written waiver before the firm proceeds.
